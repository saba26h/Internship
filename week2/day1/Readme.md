# Day 1 - Descriptive Statistics 📊

## Overview

The first day focused on learning the fundamentals of Descriptive Statistics and understanding how statistical measures are used to summarize and describe data.

The goal was to learn how to identify the center of a dataset, measure its variability, understand the effect of outliers, and interpret statistical results before moving to Machine Learning modeling.

---

## Topics Covered

### Measures of Central Tendency

I learned how to describe the center of a dataset using different statistical measures.

The topics included:

* Calculating the Mean (Arithmetic Average).
* Finding the Median (Middle Value).
* Identifying the Mode (Most Frequent Value).
* Choosing the appropriate measure based on the dataset.
* Understanding how outliers affect the mean and median differently.

---

### Measures of Spread

I learned how to measure the variability and distribution of data.

The topics included:

* Calculating the Range.
* Understanding Variance.
* Computing Standard Deviation.
* Calculating the Interquartile Range (IQR).
* Comparing datasets based on their spread.

---

### Percentiles and Quartiles

I learned how percentiles and quartiles help describe the distribution of data.

The topics included:

* Understanding Percentiles.
* Finding the First Quartile (Q1).
* Finding the Median (Q2).
* Finding the Third Quartile (Q3).
* Calculating the Interquartile Range (IQR).
* Using quartiles to understand data distribution.

---

### Describing a Real Dataset

I learned how to apply descriptive statistics to analyze a real dataset.

The topics included:

* Loading numerical data using NumPy and Pandas.
* Calculating mean, median, and mode.
* Computing standard deviation and IQR.
* Comparing the mean and median to evaluate the effect of outliers.
* Summarizing statistical findings in plain language.

## Key Takeaways

By the end of this day, I learned how to summarize datasets using descriptive statistics, calculate measures of central tendency and spread, and interpret statistical results correctly.

These skills provide a strong foundation for exploratory data analysis and will be useful for future Data Science and Machine Learning projects.
